StaffPulse is the proactive burnout prevention platform designed for modern remote and hybrid teams. 

Empower your team with 10-second anonymous daily pulse check-ins that protect employee psychological safety while giving managers the predictive visibility they need to stop burnout before it leads to turnover.

WHY STAFFPULSE?
• 76% of employees experience burnout, but fear speaking up due to performance evaluation concerns.
• Traditional quarterly HR surveys are too slow, too long, and suffer from low response rates.

KEY FEATURES:

• Daily rotating HMAC-SHA256 tokens ensure employee responses can never be traced back to an individual name, email, or hardware ID.

⚡ 10-Second Daily Check-ins
• Seamless 4-step check-in capturing Mood, Energy level, and Workload pressure.

📊 Proactive Manager Dashboard
• Real-time Team Wellness Score (0–100) computed from our weighted clinical wellbeing formula.
• Early Burnout Warning Gauge alerting managers when stress thresholds exceed safe boundaries.
