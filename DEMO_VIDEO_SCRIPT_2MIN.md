# StaffPulse 2-Minute Demo Video Script

> **Target Length:** 115 – 120 seconds (Max 2:00 per Ship-a-ton rules)  
> **Format:** Screen recording of device / emulator with voiceover narration.

---

### Segment 1: The Hook & Problem (0:00 – 0:25)
- **Visual:** Open on the StaffPulse splash & Onboarding screen showing "100% Anonymous Daily Pulse Check-in".
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"76% of employees experience burnout, but most stay silent because they fear being judged or penalized. Managers only find out when their best performers abruptly hand in their resignation.  
  > Meet **StaffPulse** — the burnout prevention app that creates psychological safety for teams through 10-second anonymous pulse check-ins and predictive Pro analytics powered by RevenueCat."*

---

### Segment 2: The 10-Second Employee Check-in (0:25 – 0:50)
- **Visual:** Tap "Employee / Team Member", tap "Start Check-in". Rapidly click:
  - Mood: "Good" 🙂
  - Energy: "High Energy" ⚡
  - Workload: "Balanced" ⚖️
  - Tap "Submit Check-in" → Show Success Screen with the Harvard Business Review quote.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"For employees, it takes literally 10 seconds. You tap your mood, energy level, and workload. Individual submissions are cryptographically hashed and never linked to your identity. Employees get a private wellbeing score, and their response immediately feeds into aggregate team insights."*

---

### Segment 3: The Manager Dashboard & Early Burnout Detection (0:50 – 1:20)
- **Visual:** Switch to Manager Role / Dashboard.
  - Highlight the "Team Wellness Score" (e.g. 72/100).
  - Show the "Burnout Risk Meter" (e.g., Green / Low Risk).
  - Scroll down through the 7-day interactive trend chart (`fl_chart`).
  - Show the minimum 3-response aggregation safeguard badge.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"On the Manager Dashboard, leaders see real-time team wellness trajectories without ever violating individual privacy. The early burnout detection meter warns managers two weeks before a crash happens, and the 7-day trend shows whether stress is an isolated spike or systemic team fatigue."*

---

### Segment 4: Monetization & RevenueCat Paywall Flow (1:20 – 1:45)
- **Visual:** 
  - Tap "Export PDF Report" or the Pro Upgrade Banner.
  - Present the **Paywall Screen**.
  - Show the Annual plan with the "Save 33%" badge, feature comparison, and the mandatory "Restore Purchases" button.
  - Tap "Start Pro" → Show instant activation!
  - Tap "Export PDF" → Show the generated branded Executive PDF Report preview.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Monetization is seamlessly driven by RevenueCat. Free teams can monitor up to 3 members. As teams grow, managers upgrade to StaffPulse Pro for $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year.  
  > Powered by RevenueCat entitlements, Pro unlocks 30-day deep analytics, smart burnout alerts, and one-tap executive PDF wellness reports with actionable manager intervention plans."*

---

### Segment 5: Call to Action & Wrap-up (1:45 – 2:00)
- **Visual:** Back to the dashboard with the Pro badge glowing, then display the StaffPulse logo with the RevenueCat & Samsung Galaxy Store badges.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"StaffPulse bridges the trust gap between employees and managers, preventing burnout before it costs companies their best talent. Built with Flutter, Firebase, and RevenueCat for the 2026 Ship-a-ton. Thank you!"*
